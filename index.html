Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you buy — and everything that happens after.

Will this work on my phone?

Lumi works on eSIM-capable phones. That includes iPhone XS and newer, and most Android flagship phones released after 2020 (Samsung Galaxy S20+, Google Pixel 3+, and many others). When you tap "Get eSIM", we check your device compatibility before payment — so there are no surprises.

How fast will I receive my eSIM?

Your eSIM is prepared as soon as payment is confirmed. You'll receive confirmation by email and Instagram DM with your eSIM profile and simple installation steps — usually within 2 minutes.

Which networks do you support?

We currently support Zong, Telenor, and Jazz. You choose your preferred network on each package before checkout.

Is there a QR code?

No. Unlike most eSIM providers, we install your profile directly on your phone through a secure digital handshake. You don't scan anything.

What if my eSIM doesn't work?

If your eSIM fails to activate due to a technical fault on our end, you receive a full refund. Please see our refund policy for the complete terms.

What about PTA registration and device blocking?

PTA blocks phones that aren't biometrically registered after 60 days of use. eSIMs are a practical workaround for short trips and data use — because the eSIM isn't tied to your device IMEI in the same way a physical SIM is. This is especially useful for tourists, business travellers, and Pakistanis returning for short visits.

For long-term use inside Pakistan, please check PTA's latest regulations. The current policy (as of August 2026) allows up to 10 free eSIM transfers with an initial issuance fee capped at Rs 1,500.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. If our eSIM fails to activate due to a fault on our side, we refund you in full. Refunds are processed within 3–5 working days. See our refund policy.

Can I use this in a different country?

Our packages are for use inside Pakistan. If you're travelling abroad, contact us on Instagram for guidance.

How do I contact support?

The fastest way is Instagram DM: @lumiesim.store. You can also email us at support@lumiesim.store. We respond 24/7.
